Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2025 | Effective Date: November 3, 2025

Welcome to Stellvia ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services (collectively, the "Services").

This policy complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other applicable data protection laws.

By using Stellvia, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and authentication credentials
  • Profile Data: Financial goals, preferences, persona selection, and onboarding responses
  • Financial Information: Transaction descriptions (no bank account numbers), receipt images and parsed data, budget goals and spending categories, cases and disputes you create
  • Communications: Messages, support requests, and feedback you send us
  • Payment Information: Subscription and payment details processed through Apple App Store / Google Play Store and RevenueCat (we do not store credit card numbers)

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Features used, screens viewed, actions taken, session duration
  • Location Data: Approximate location for fuel price alerts (only when permission granted)
  • Log Data: Error logs, crash reports, and performance metrics

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Providers: Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In (email, name, profile picture)
  • Payment Processors: RevenueCat (subscription status, purchase history)
  • Cloud Services: Firebase, Google Cloud Platform (service operations)

2. How We Use Your Information

To Provide and Improve Services

  • Deliver core features (receipt scanning, case management, financial advice)
  • Process AI-powered analysis using OpenAI GPT-4
  • Personalize your experience based on your persona and goals
  • Provide fuel price alerts and savings recommendations
  • Generate legal letters and documents for disputes

Communication

  • Send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset)
  • Deliver in-app notifications and alerts
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Send service updates (you can opt-out)

✓

To exercise these rights: Email us at privacy@stellvia.co

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication with secure token management
  • Access Controls: Role-based access, principle of least privilege
  • Monitoring: Real-time security monitoring and alerts

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Retention Periods:

  • Active account data: Indefinite (until account deletion)
  • Receipt images: 7 years (tax compliance)
  • Case documents: 5 years (legal requirements)
  • Analytics data: 26 months
  • Log data: 90 days
  • Deleted account data: 30 days (backup retention)
