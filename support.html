❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do credits work?

Credits let you use AI-powered features. Each subscription includes monthly credits: 100 (Starter), 200 (Pro), or 400 (Family). Unused credits don't roll over.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android) → Subscriptions → Stellvia → Cancel. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple/Google per their policies. Contact App Store or Play Store support directly for refund requests.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days (POPIA compliance).

How do I export my data?

In the app: Settings → Data Export. You can download all your personal information in JSON format (POPIA right to access).

Is my receipt data private?

Yes! Text recognition happens on your device. We only store anonymized swap patterns to improve suggestions. Receipt images are encrypted.

How accurate is the AI advice?

Our AI uses GPT-4 and South African legal/financial data. However, AI-generated content is educational only - consult a professional for serious matters.

What if I find a scam or price error?

Report it in the Shield tab! Community reports help protect everyone. Verified scams are shared with SABRIC.

🇿🇦 POPIA Compliance

Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) Compliance

Stellvia fully complies with South Africa's POPIA regulations. You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information (Data Export)
  • Correct inaccurate information (Edit Profile)
  • Delete your information (Delete Account)
  • Restrict processing (Consent Manager)
  • Receive your data in portable format (JSON export)
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Information Officer: privacy@stellvia.co
